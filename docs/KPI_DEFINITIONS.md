# KPI Definitions

The following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are tracked by NOIPMP to measure operational health and platform ROI.

### Mean Time To Acknowledge (MTTA)
The average time elapsed between an initial monitoring alert firing and an engineer officially acknowledging the correlated incident ticket in the system.

### Mean Time To Diagnose (MTTD)
The average time elapsed between incident creation and the successful generation/attachment of a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) artifact. Because NOIPMP automates this step, this metric is expected to drop significantly.

### Mean Time To Resolve (MTTR)
The average time elapsed between incident creation and the final resolution timestamp recorded by an engineer, indicating the service is fully restored and validated.

### First-Time Resolution Rate
The percentage of incidents resolved without being reopened or recurring on the same device within a 24-hour window. A higher rate indicates that the automated RCA and KB SOPs are accurate and effective.

### Predictive Tickets
The total count of proactive maintenance tickets generated by the Machine Learning (Random Forest) model for devices exceeding the high-risk threshold, prior to a hard failure.

### Incidents Prevented
A strict metric counting predictive tickets where:
1. A preventive action was actively logged and executed.
2. The predicted failure window passed without an actual downtime event occurring.
*(Note: The system will never automatically claim an incident was prevented without concrete evidence).*

### KB Reuse Rate
The frequency with which an automatically generated Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is attached or referenced in subsequent, similar incidents by L1/L2 engineers.

### RCA Automation Rate
The percentage of total network incidents that are successfully processed by the deterministic rules engine, resulting in an automated RCA, versus incidents that required manual CLI parsing by an engineer.
