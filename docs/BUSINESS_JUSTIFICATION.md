# Business Justification & Measurement Framework

## Value Proposition
The NOIPMP platform shifts network operations from a reactive cost-center to a proactive, automated intelligence function. By accelerating diagnostics through deterministic RCA and predicting failures before they impact business services, the platform drives efficiency and reliability. 

Importantly, NOIPMP avoids arbitrary claims of monetary savings or percentage improvements prior to deployment. Instead, the platform includes a strict measurement framework to calculate actual, provable return on investment (ROI) post-deployment.

## Measurement Framework for Calculating Savings

To calculate actual operational savings after deployment, organizations must track the following baselines before rollout and compare them against post-deployment platform telemetry:

### 1. Incident Resolution Cost Savings
* **Formula**: `(Historical Baseline MTTR - Platform MTTR) * Fully Loaded Engineer Hourly Rate * Number of Incidents`
* **Data Source**: Platform KPI Dashboard vs. Historical ITSM Data.
* **Objective**: Measure the exact cost reduction achieved by shortening the triage and diagnostic lifecycle.

### 2. Tier-Level Escalation Deflection
* **Formula**: `(Historical L3 Escalation % - Platform L3 Escalation %) * Total Incidents * Delta Cost between L3 and L1/L2 effort`
* **Data Source**: ITSM ticket assignee histories cross-referenced with KB Draft usage.
* **Objective**: Measure the savings generated by enabling L1/L2 engineers to handle complex incidents using evidence-based SOPs.

### 3. Downtime Prevention Savings
* **Formula**: `Confirmed Prevented Incidents * Average Cost of Downtime per Incident`
* **Data Source**: Incidents are strictly marked as "Prevented" ONLY if a preventive action was executed during the predictive warning window, and the predicted failure did not occur.
* **Objective**: Quantify the revenue or productivity saved by addressing hardware or configuration degradation proactively.

### 4. Knowledge Creation Efficiency
* **Formula**: `Number of Automated KB Drafts Approved * Historical Average Time to Write SOP * Engineer Hourly Rate`
* **Data Source**: Platform KB System (Version History logs).
* **Objective**: Calculate the operational hours saved by automatically generating structured documentation from RCA artifacts.
